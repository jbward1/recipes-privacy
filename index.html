Recipes – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Recipes does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Your recipes stay on your device

Recipes you import, their photos and the app's database are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

How recipe files are read

PDFs and photos you import are read on your device. Where available, Apple's on-device model helps identify ingredients and steps. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party.

No tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and makes no network requests.

Changes

If a future version adds features that send data off your device, such as cloud sync, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
